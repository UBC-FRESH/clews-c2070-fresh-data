## Caveats

- All values are model estimates; `observed=false` and `policy_grade=false`.
- The 119 Mm3 case is a planning allocation, not a legal AAC, a sustainable-supply determination, or observed harvest.
- Assignment v6 applies the 1.2x provincial floor by scaling proxy capacity only. Scaled capacity is not observed capacity.
- Process residue totals are invariant; v6 changes their province/facility distribution through local-first allocation.
- Zone PJ splits use the fixed handoff-v4 proxy weights. They are not observed sub-provincial residue shares.
- No UI or public OSeCAN surface is changed.
