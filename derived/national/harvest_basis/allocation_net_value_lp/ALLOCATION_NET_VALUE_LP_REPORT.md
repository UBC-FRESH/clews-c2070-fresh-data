## Method

The canonical allocation remains the least-deviation allocation from observed 2024 provincial shares. This sensitivity keeps that baseline and solves a separate LP for each price scenario and national ceiling:

```text
maximize  sum_p h_p * margin_base_p
subject to sum_p h_p = T
           0 <= h_p <= upper_bound_p
```

The documented-cap cases use the canonical documented ceilings. The intensity cases use the canonical operable-proxy screen `upper_bound_p = min(documented cap, I * operable_proxy_ha)`. Base-margin unit values are distinct across provinces in each scenario, so the net-value optimum is unique; no secondary tie-break is needed. The allocation is optimized on the central (`base`) margin; the same allocation is then re-priced at the low and high margins. Low/high are uncertainty bounds, not separately optimized allocations.

## Caveats

- donor_flat is a donor/flat price proxy, not an observed provincial price; stumpage_scaled is a method sensitivity. Both are model_estimate, observed=false, policy_grade=false, and not policy-grade net value.
- Negative margins are retained unclipped. When total harvest exceeds the capacity of positive-margin provinces, the LP necessarily allocates to negative-margin provinces; it chooses the least-negative available margin.
- The operable-proxy landbase is a SCANFI merchantability screen intersected with a share-based 2016 tenure proxy. It is not a legal THLB: it carries no slope, road access, age/disturbance, growth, yield, or economic constraint. Intensity caps are screening sensitivities only.
- The objective is a static unit-margin times harvest sum. It has no demand, mill capacity, tenure, haul-distance, species, employment, silviculture, or discounting content.
- The net-value allocation is a sensitivity, not a policy allocation, not a forecast, and not a legal AAC. The canonical least-deviation allocation is unchanged and remains the model of record.
- The documented-cap sum is a mixed-basis ceiling set, not a national AAC. PE and NU have no operable proxy and are flagged operable_missing, not assigned zero area.
- Net value low/base/high re-prices the central-margin allocation at the v2.3 low/base/high margins; low and high are uncertainty bounds, not separately optimized allocations.
