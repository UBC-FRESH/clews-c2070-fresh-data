1.       INTRODUCTION

The purpose of this paper is to provide an overview of the July 1, 2025, update to the Interior
Market Pricing System (MPS). 1

2.       AUCTION DATASET

The new auction dataset used in the update contains winning bids and data from 3,342 sales over
the 15-year period from January 1, 2010, through December 31, 2024.

The 2025 dataset spans 15 years of timber sale data (2010–2024), following the adoption of a
rolling average in the previous modelling year (2024). Further to dropping (2009) and adding a
year of data (2024), TPB also made the following changes:

     •    For the Partial Cut 20 variable, the calculation method has changed. Previously, values
          from all blocks were averaged first, and then the 20% partial cut retention rule was
          applied. Now, the 20% rule is applied first at the harvest method level, and the results are
          then averaged using harvest method volumes as weights.


Timber Pricing Branch, Ministry of Forests (July 1, 2025)                                                2

    •    The harvest-method volume-weighted slope variable is now calculated in the same way
         as partial cut 20. The slope adjustment (e.g., max(slope – 15, 0)) is applied first at the
         harvest method level, and the results are then averaged using harvest method volumes.

    •    The CAMP variable has been improved by reducing the search radius from 8 km to 3 km,
         which enhanced its performance and statistical significance.

    •    The CYCLE variable has been simplified. Previously, it was modified by adding half of
         the hours exceeding 6 to the original cycle time to force a hinge (using a piecewise
         function). Now, it is reported as the raw cycle time in hours, without the application of
         hinges.

    •    Finally, prices for zone 9 are now based on a volume-weighted average of prices from
         both zones 5 and 9, while zone 5 prices remain unchanged.

                                                      APPENDIX 1

                             DESCRIPTION OF SPECIFIED OPERATIONS

If sufficient auction data is not available for an activity employed by either BCTS or other
licences, the ministry may, for those identified situations, implement a specified operations cost
estimate in the calculation of the stumpage rate.

The specified operations will be used to adjust the MPS stumpage rate for the estimated
incremental cost of the identified situation. The explicit assumption is that if a bidder was faced
with a similar situation he or she would lower the bid by the extra cost incurred because of the
identified situation.

The situations that may be eligible for specified operations adjustment will be determined
according to the following principles:

    •    the expectation that a bid would be influenced by this situation;
    •    representation (number of samples, if any, in the auction data set);
    •    materiality of estimated cost differential (supported by verifiable financial data); and,
    •    statistical analysis (including the premise that other represented situations and variables
         in the MPS database and equations may serve as a proxy for the situation in question).

The ministry, after considering the above and any other relevant technical information, may or
may not designate the situation as an identified situation eligible for specified operations and, if
eligible, will specify the dollars per cubic meter adjustment.

The ultimate objective is to have a representative auction database and hence, few, if any,
specified operations adjustments.

The following are identified as specified operations for the Interior MPS. Cost estimates from the
updated Interior Appraisal Manual are used for the following:

    •    Rail Haul
    •    Rail haul including truck to rail transfer and rail transport.
    •    Barge/Ferry
    •    Barge/ferry used to truck haul (private).
    •    Barge/ferry not used for truck haul (private).
    •    Dump and boom
    •    Tow
    •    Dewater and reload
    •    Skyline Yarding
    •    Horse Logging
    •    Market Logger Specified Operations Cost
    •    High Development Cost (BCTS only)
    •    Helicopter Logging
